# Tata GenAI Powered Data Analytics Simulation

A completed **Tata GenAI Powered Data Analytics Job Simulation** conducted by **Tata iQ in collaboration with Forage**, focused on using **GenAI, data analytics, predictive modeling, and responsible AI** to address customer delinquency in the financial services sector.

---

## 🚀 Overview

In this virtual job simulation, I worked as an **AI and Analytics Consultant** for **Geldium**, a fictional financial services organization.

The objective was to design a **data-driven and GenAI-powered approach** for predicting customer delinquency and developing targeted collections strategies.

The simulation involved:

* Exploring and interpreting **customer financial data**
* Identifying important **delinquency risk indicators**
* Designing a **predictive modeling framework**
* Developing **targeted business interventions**
* Designing an **autonomous AI-powered collections strategy**
* Considering **responsible AI, fairness, explainability, and regulatory requirements**

A key component of the simulation was designing an AI solution that could support collections while maintaining responsible decision-making.

* **Fairness**

* **Transparency**
* **Human oversight**
* **Data privacy**
